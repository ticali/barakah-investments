What We Stand For

Our Guiding Principles

Every business we build or invest in is guided by the same simple conviction: doing things the right way — with integrity, excellence, and purpose — is also the most durable path to success.

🌿

Halal by Design

We don't retrofit ethics onto business — we build it in from day one. Every operation, partnership, and dollar earned meets halal standards, because what we do should be something we're proud to stand behind.

⭐

Excellence as Differentiation

Mediocre is everywhere. We back businesses that obsess over the details — the quality of the product, the warmth of the service, the experience customers walk away talking about.

🤝

Value for Everyone

Sustainable businesses create win-wins. We look for ventures where customers genuinely benefit, employees are treated with dignity, and the business thrives as a result of both.

The Founder

Ali Shaikh

Ali Shaikh is the founder and managing partner of Barakah Investments Group, an LLC dedicated to building and backing halal businesses across the DMV area and beyond.

With a background spanning technology, operations, and entrepreneurship, Ali brings a hands-on approach to every venture — from concept development through day-to-day execution. He believes that ethical business and profitable business are not in tension; they reinforce each other.

Through Barakah, Ali is building a portfolio of brands that reflect his conviction: that Muslim entrepreneurs and consumers deserve options that don't ask them to compromise on what matters most.
